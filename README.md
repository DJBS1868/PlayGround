Build your dream Lightsaber piece-by-piece across 9 custom categories—choosing your exact emitters, sleeves, crystals, and ignition effects—then test your blade in a zero-gravity Disassembly Bay. Once forged, cash in on your craftsmanship at the Coruscant Exchange Auction House as rival bidders fight over your custom designs. When you're ready for action, dive into the Activities menu: slash through droids and targets in the arcade Scrap Run minigame, or test your skills in the tactical Holocron Duel Simulator to climb the combat ranks!

Key Features Explaied: 
 * Deep 3D Workbench Customization: Forge your custom hilt piece-by-piece across 9 modular categories (emitters, grips, crystals, and switches) with real-time blade ignition and visual testing.
 * Coruscant Exchange Auction House: Take your finished creations to a live auction floor where competing galactic collectors bid on your designs based on rarity and craftsmanship.
 * Disassembly Bay: Inspect the inner workings of your weapons in a zero-gravity, exploded-part view that breaks down every component and glowing Kyber crystal.
 * Scrap Run Arcade Minigame: Test your blade edge in a fast-paced slicing challenge, slashing through flying droids, cargo, and salvage to rack up score multipliers.
 * Holocron Duel Simulator: Take your vault of custom sabers into tactical combat against AI holograms, utilizing strategic stances, blocks, and counter-attacks to climb the combat ranks.
